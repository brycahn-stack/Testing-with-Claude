## Voice Journal

An Apple Watch + iPhone app for frictionless voice journaling: tap the mic on your
watch, speak a thought, and the phone transcribes it, figures out what kind of
thought it was (business idea, task, meeting, workout, meal, note), and files it in
the right place — Reminders, Calendar, or a typed log — automatically.

See [`VoiceJournal/README.md`](VoiceJournal/README.md) for architecture, build
instructions, and the roadmap.
